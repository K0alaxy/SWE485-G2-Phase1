Original Purpose:
    This dataset was created by IBM data scientists to study what drives employees to leave a company.
    It contains 1,470 employee records with 35 features covering demographics, job details, satisfaction
    scores, and work history. The goal was to help HR teams identify patterns behind employee attrition
    and build predictive models to flag at-risk employees before they quit.
